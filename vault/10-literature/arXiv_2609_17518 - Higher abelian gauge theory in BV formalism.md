# Higher abelian gauge theory in BV formalism

## Abstract

> A BV structure is constructed on the space of zero modes of a quadratic elliptic higher abelian
> gauge theory. The associated BV integral is shown to be independent of the choice of zero-mode
> Lagrangian under mild hypotheses. The construction yields explicit zero-mode partition functions
> for several $p$-form abelian gauge theories.
