# Mass gap for the hierarchical sinh-Gordon model

## Abstract

> The (massless) sinh-Gordon model is defined in terms of a continuum massless Gaussian free field
> perturbed by a cosh interaction. For the hierarchical version of the model, we prove existence
> of the infinite volume limit, a uniform log-Sobolev inequality, and a mass gap. Our results hold
> for all $b^2 \in (0,1)$ and simplify for $b^2 \in (0,1/2)$ for which the Gaussian multiplicative
> chaos has two moments. In an appendix, our renormalization group analysis is compared with the
> conjectures and controversies in physics which are mostly based on formal analytic continuation
> of conjectures for the sine-Gordon model and we raise some questions.
