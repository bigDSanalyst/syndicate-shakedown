# On spatial decoherence in many-body systems

## Abstract

> We study the dynamics of a many-body quantum system strongly interacting with a bosonic
> reservoir. The coupling is given by a potential operator for the system and it is linear in the
> field. We show that in the limit of infinite coupling strength the system undergoes
> instantaneous spatial decoherence. To resolve the decoherence process in time we consider large
> coupling strengths $λ$ and short times scaling as $t\propto λ^{-α}$, with $α\ge0$, in the limit
> $λ\rightarrow\infty$. On short time scales $α>1$ the dynamics is trivial while on longer ones
> $0\leα<1$ the decoherence is instantaneous. We show that the decoherence process is resolved
> exactly for $α=1$, defining the fine-grained time scale $τ=λt$. We construct an approximate
> effective evolution map of the many-body system with controlled error estimates. Generically,
> the effective dynamics is markovian, but not given by a dynamical semigroup. As a physical
> application we give a rigorous description of the phenomenon of localization of macroscopic
> quantum objects in position space.
