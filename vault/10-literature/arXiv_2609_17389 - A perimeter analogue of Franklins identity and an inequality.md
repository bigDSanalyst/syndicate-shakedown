# A perimeter analogue of Franklin's identity and an inequality related to the parity of parts

## Abstract

> We prove two conjectures regarding partition perimeter inequalities. It was conjectured by Gray,
> Payne, and Watson that Franklin's partition identity becomes an eventual inequality if one
> replaces the size of the partition with its perimeter. We prove this conjecture by asymptotic
> analysis of the corresponding generating functions. Gray, Payne, Swisher, and Watson also
> conjectured that there is a bias for partitions with fixed perimeter to have more odd parts than
> even parts. We prove this conjecture by deriving the corresponding generating functions and give
> a positive recurrence for the coefficients. In the case that there are an equal number of odd
> parts and even parts we provide a connection to peakless Motzkin paths.
