# Catalan's constant is irrational

## Abstract

> Whether the constant $$G=\sum_{k=0}^\infty\frac{(-1)^k}{(2k+1)^2}=\frac1{1^2}-
> \frac1{3^2}+\frac1{5^2}-\frac1{7^2}+\cdots$$ introduced by Catalan in the nineteen century is
> irrational, is a long-standing open problem. In this paper we prove the irrationality of $G$ via
> using suitable weights.
